I’m a Senior Software Engineer at Meta with five years of experience building extensible frameworks, shipping features to billions of users, handling complex system migrations, and unblocking developer velocity. 

As someone who loves brewing a good cup of coffee, I like to bring a similarly grounded and thoughtful approach to software development. I enjoy tackling user-oriented problems that involve diving into ambiguous technical spaces, and translating messy requirements into clean, maintainable systems. I'm driven by curiosity and a deep commitment to doing the right thing, and I do my best work on projects that require close attention to detail or navigating complex tradeoffs. I like to center my day-to-day around open communication, empathy, and a healthy dose of humor.

I graduated with a Computer Science degree from the University of Michigan in 2020. Over the last several years, I’ve been reconnecting with my childhood roots in fantasy, though I had a brief past life in sci-fi: working on a NASA challenge in college and working as an Explainer at the National Air and Space Museum while in high school.

When I’m not writing code, you’ll find me reading, playing all kinds of games, hunting for vintage clothes and vinyl records, attending fantasy balls, or training in Muay Thai.
